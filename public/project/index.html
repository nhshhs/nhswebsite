Project Growth


“What you do makes a difference, and you have to decide what kind of difference you want to make.”
- Dr. Jane Goodall
Project Growth aims to provide guidance and support to middle schoolers.
Throughout the year, members will provide guidance and advice about school, extracurriculars, internships, and more through a mentorship relationship.
Members will be paired with an underclassmen and have one-on-one mentoring sessions. Members will also be creating resource packets to assist fellow high school peers with additional resources on specific classes, extracurriculars, and careers.
Through fundraising and volunteering, we hope to contribute meaningfully to this worthy cause.
