Resource Packets

NHS members have put together a variety of resource packets covering AP classes, SAT/ACT, careers, and study tips for all high school students to use. They include a lot of valuable information, resources, first hand experiences, and tips to help people better succeed in high school. There is a variety of useful topics covered in these resource packets so be sure to check them out!
